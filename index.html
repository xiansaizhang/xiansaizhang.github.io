About me

I’m a first-year PhD candidate in economics at the Erasmus School of Economics. My research interests lie broadly in public economics and industrial organisation, with a particular focus on corporate taxation, firm behaviour and policy evaluation.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a research assistant at the Max Planck Institute for Tax Law and Public Finance. I hold a bachelor’s degree in economics from the University of Manchester and a master’s degree in quantitative economics from LMU Munich.

Work in Progress

Firm Responses to Tax Disincentives: Evidence from Denmark

Examines firms’ responses to Denmark’s 2008 depreciation allowance reform using firm-level Orbis data and a difference-in-differences research design to identify its causal effects.

Energy Support Policies and Firm Responses in the Netherlands

with Enrico Pennings and Jolanda Hessels

Using firm-level microdata from Statistics Netherlands (CBS), this study aims to identify the causal effects of the 2022 Dutch energy-support measure by exploiting its eligibility threshold as a source of quasi-experimental variation, while also examining the determinants of subsidy take-up as a secondary research objective.
